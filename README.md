# Witness Priority Mapper DI² — Civil-Litigation Demo

This repository preserves a 12-page external-safe licensing demo for Grounded DI LLC. It demonstrates source-linked witness prioritization and exposure-gap review for a hypothetical industrial asbestos/product-liability investigation.

## Demonstrated workflow

The PDF records a claim-discipline statement, source register, chronology, product/exposure gap matrix, witness-role classifier, and lead evaluation. The workflow routes investigators toward the highest-value former coworkers, supervisors, tradespeople, and document sources before search resources are spent.

The sample result is **targeted witness-location escalation with attorney review**. The social-media-only lead `W-001` remains an **UNVERIFIED investigative lead**; identity, employment, site overlap, and willingness must be confirmed before reliance.

## What it demonstrates

- Separates source reliability from allegations and follow-up needs.
- Prioritizes gaps such as exact site, employer, unit, product, component, asbestos content, frequency, proximity, and maintenance activity.
- Distinguishes what each witness category may support from what it cannot prove alone.
- Preserves attorney review for legal strategy and litigation-facing conclusions.

## Scope and limitations

All facts are hypothetical. The artifact contains no real client or privileged case information and does not prove exposure, asbestos content, causation, punitive damages, liability, or legal responsibility. It is a litigation-support workflow demonstration, not automated legal resolution or independent validation.
